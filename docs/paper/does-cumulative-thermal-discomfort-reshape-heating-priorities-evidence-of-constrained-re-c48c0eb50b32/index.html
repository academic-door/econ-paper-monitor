累积热不适是否会重塑供暖优先级？法国家庭受限顺从的证据

Does cumulative thermal discomfort reshape heating priorities? Evidence of constrained resignation among French households

Elisabeth Bourgeois

摘要

Household energy decisions are typically modeled as linear responses to climatic conditions, overlooking the behavioral responses to prolonged climatic stress. A cumulative Thermal Discomfort Index (TDI) is introduced to capture cold stress through an asymmetric, deviation-based design. Unlike Heating Degree Days, which proxy heating requirements from mean temperature alone, the TDI integrates multidimensional deviations from local climatic norms over three winters and yields the lowest AIC among eleven benchmark indices tested on the same specification. Drawing on a survey of 3874 French households and spatial econometric methods, the analysis examines stated heating priorities, a self-reported trade-off between thermal comfort and energy savings, rather than metered consumption. The results document a non-monotonic behavioral pattern: heightened cumulative discomfort does not strengthen the stated preference for warmth but is associated with a shift toward energy savings prioritization. Cumulative TDI enters with the expected signs, positive on savings prioritization and negative on comfort, but neither pooled coefficient is statistically distinguishable from zero ( p = 0.42 and p = 0.75 ), and a likelihood ratio test against a controls-only specification does not reject ( p = 0.133 ). No benchmark climatic index reaches conventional significance on this sample either. Individual effect sizes are small per unit and accumulate only across the full observed range of climatic exposure: from minimum to maximum TDI (77.9 units) the implied shift reaches approximately 7 percentage points toward savings, while between the 5th and 95th percentiles (28.5 units) it is 2.6 points. Sub-sample stratification points to structural concentration in point estimates: the TDI coefficient is roughly two and a half times as large in high-stress regions, although the difference between sub-samples does not reach conventional significance ( z = − 0.43 ). This regional contrast separates the proposed mechanism from a pure budget-constraint account, which would predict the sharper response in colder regions where heating needs and bills are highest; the larger point estimates arise instead where cold is unexpected and dwellings are structurally unprepared, a response to violated climatic expectations rather than to chronic heating costs or income constraints alone. Moran’s I diagnostics on the model residuals ( I = 0.015 , p ≈ 0.75 ) indicate that the behavioral shift is associated with household-level constraints, building inefficiency and income precariousness, rather than geographic contagion. These findings support reorienting energy policy from bill-based subsidies toward removing the structural barriers that lock vulnerable households into involuntary thermal deprivation. The TDI offers a diagnostic tool for identifying populations where climate adaptation may have become a source of behavioral constraint rather than resilience.

中文摘要

家庭能源决策通常被建模为对气候条件的线性响应，忽视了长期气候压力下的行为反应。本文引入累积热不适指数（TDI），通过非对称的、基于偏差的设计来捕捉寒冷压力。与仅依据平均温度代理供暖需求的供暖度日不同，TDI整合了三个冬季相对于当地气候规范的多维偏差，并在同一设定下测试的十一个基准指数中产生最低的AIC。基于对3874户法国家庭的调查和空间计量方法，分析考察了陈述性供暖优先级，即自报的热舒适与节能之间的权衡，而非计量消费。结果记录了一种非单调的行为模式：累积不适加剧并不会强化对温暖的陈述性偏好，而是与向节能优先的转变相关。累积TDI以预期符号进入模型，对节能优先为正、对舒适为负，但两个混合系数均无法在统计上与零区分（p = 0.42和p = 0.75），且相对于仅含控制变量设定的似然比检验未能拒绝（p = 0.133）。在这一样本中，也没有任何基准气候指数达到常规显著性。个体效应量每单位很小，且仅在气候暴露的完整观测范围内累积：从TDI最小值到最大值（77.9个单位），隐含的向节能转变约为7个百分点，而在第5百分位与第95百分位之间（28.5个单位）则为2.6个百分点。子样本分层表明点估计存在结构性集中：在高压力地区，TDI系数大约为其他地区的2.5倍，尽管子样本之间的差异未达到常规显著性（z = −0.43）。这种区域对比将所提出的机制与纯粹的预算约束解释区分开来，后者会预测在供暖需求和账单最高的较冷地区出现更强烈的响应；相反，较大的点估计出现在寒冷出乎意料且住房在结构上未做好准备的地区，这是对气候预期被违背的响应，而非仅对长期供暖成本或收入约束的响应。模型残差的Moran’s I诊断（I = 0.015，p ≈ 0.75）表明，这种行为转变与家庭层面约束、建筑低效和收入不稳定相关，而非地理传染。这些发现支持将能源政策从基于账单的补贴转向消除结构性障碍，这些障碍使脆弱家庭陷入非自愿的热剥夺。TDI提供了一种诊断工具，用于识别那些气候适应可能已成为行为约束来源而非韧性来源的人群。
