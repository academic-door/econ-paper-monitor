引擎运转，质量提升：能源稳定供应对中国出口产品质量的影响研究

Engine Run, Quality Enhance: Examining the Effects of Energy Steady Supply on Chinese Export Product Quality

Yao Hu, Shan Shao, Hui Wang, Weifen Lin

摘要

Oil is the lifeblood of industry, serving as the core source of power for the operation of industrial machinery. Ensuring its stable supply is crucial for the smooth production process, which in turn is a key determinant in enhancing the quality of export products for enterprises. This study empirically examines the impact of energy supply on the quality of export products and the mechanism of its effect by using a staggered difference‐in‐differences (DID) method with the implementation of China's West‐to‐East Oil Transportation Project as a quasi‐natural experiment. The empirical results show that a dependable energy supply substantially boosts the quality of goods produced for export by various companies. The mechanism analysis shows that energy supply improves export product quality through enterprise performance improvement effects, industry agglomeration effects and supply chain stabilization effects, and it mainly improves export product quality in energy‐importing regions, technology‐intensive industries, private enterprises, and general trade enterprises. Further analysis reveals that a stable supply of energy helps enhance the sustainability of enterprise exports while helping enterprises realize value chain upgrading, helping China get rid of the curse of locking the bottom of the export value chain. The findings of this study help clarify the intrinsic mechanism between energy supply and export product quality and provide an empirical basis for the cross‐regional energy dispatch strategy to help China's export value chain upgrade.

中文摘要

摘要 石油是工业的血液，是工业机械运转的核心动力来源。确保其稳定供应对于生产过程的顺利进行至关重要，而这又是提升企业出口产品质量的关键决定因素。本研究以中国“西油东送”工程实施为准自然实验，采用交错双重差分法实证检验了能源供应对出口产品质量的影响及其作用机制。实证结果表明，可靠的能源供应显著提升了各类企业的出口产品质量。机制分析显示，能源供应通过企业绩效提升效应、产业集聚效应和供应链稳定效应改善了出口产品质量，并且主要提升了能源输入地区、技术密集型行业、民营企业和一般贸易企业的出口产品质量。进一步分析发现，稳定的能源供应有助于增强企业出口的可持续性，同时帮助企业实现价值链升级，助力中国摆脱出口价值链低端锁定的困境。本研究结论有助于厘清能源供应与出口产品质量之间的内在机理，并为跨区域能源调度策略助力中国出口价值链升级提供经验依据。
