马拉维南部非洲植被覆盖变化及其治理方式的时空分析

Spatio-temporal analysis of vegetation cover transitions and underlying governance approaches in Malawi, Southern Africa

Ida Nadia S. Djenontin, Dan Wanyama, Leo C. Zulu

摘要

Forest-based solutions, such as forest landscape restoration (FLR), are increasingly emphasized in response to the interrelated crises of deforestation, biodiversity loss, and climate change. However, there is notable lack of attention to the relationship between forest governance approaches and forest transitions. In many African countries, little is known about how various forest governance models affect changes in vegetation cover over time. Leveraging remote sensing techniques and time-series analysis, we examine the changing patterns of vegetation cover from 2003 to 2021 across Malawi, where distinct governance approaches have been implemented within and across protected areas and communal lands. The analysis revealed varying patterns and magnitudes of greening (vegetation gain) and browning (vegetation loss) across different periods, seasons, locations, and scales. Our findings indicate a significant increase in greenness during 2003–2009 in districts where community-based forest management (CBFM) programs were implemented. Conversely, the extent of vegetation greening was mixed during 2005–2014 in areas where combined CBFM and forest co-management (FCoM) were the primary participatory forest governance models implemented. The FCoM governance model implemented solely during 2014–2019 was associated with very little vegetation greening. Our study offers insights into vegetation cover changes over specific timeframes, with a deeper understanding of how different participatory forest governance models have influenced these transitions. Specifically, our evidence demonstrates a positive association of CBFM with improvements in vegetation cover. This underscores the importance of strongly centering local communities in the implementation and monitoring processes of forest-based solutions, with increased power devolution, empowered self-governance authority, and stronger resource ownership rights. Consequently, an effective implementation of Malawi's FLR strategy should prioritize institutional models that fully devolve power to local community with adequate means for self-governance while granting secure resource rights. However, additional factors that would sustain observed greening outcomes require further investigation using modeling approaches.

中文摘要

基于森林的解决方案，如森林景观恢复（FLR），正日益受到重视，以应对森林砍伐、生物多样性丧失和气候变化这三重相互关联的危机。然而，森林治理方法与森林转型之间的关系却明显缺乏关注。在许多非洲国家，不同森林治理模式如何影响植被覆盖随时间的变化，人们知之甚少。利用遥感技术和时间序列分析，我们考察了2003年至2021年间马拉维植被覆盖的变化模式，该国在保护区和社区土地内外实施了不同的治理方法。分析揭示了不同时期、季节、地点和尺度上绿化（植被增加）和褐化（植被减少）的不同模式和程度。我们的研究结果表明，在实施社区森林管理（CBFM）项目的地区，2003年至2009年间绿度显著增加。相反，在2005年至2014年间，以CBFM和森林共同管理（FCoM）为主要参与式森林治理模式的地区，植被绿化的程度参差不齐。仅在2014年至2019年间实施的FCoM治理模式与极少的植被绿化相关。我们的研究提供了特定时间框架内植被覆盖变化的见解，并深入理解了不同参与式森林治理模式如何影响这些转型。具体而言，我们的证据表明CBFM与植被覆盖改善之间存在正相关关系。这强调了在森林解决方案的实施和监测过程中，以地方社区为核心的重要性，包括增加权力下放、增强自治权以及强化资源所有权。因此，有效实施马拉维的FLR战略应优先考虑将权力完全下放给地方社区、提供充分自治手段并保障资源权利的体制模式。然而，维持所观察到的绿化成果的其他因素仍需通过建模方法进一步研究。
