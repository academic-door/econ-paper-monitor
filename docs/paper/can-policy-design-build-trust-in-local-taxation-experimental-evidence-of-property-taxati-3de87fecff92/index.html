政策设计能否建立对地方税收的信任？墨西哥市政财产税的实验证据

Can policy design build trust in local taxation? Experimental evidence of property taxation in Mexican municipalities

Aurora Echavarria

摘要

Property taxes, the primary source of local revenue for municipal governments, are essential for improving urban service provision and resident welfare. Yet this tax is widely unpopular and often politically contentiuous. This study's descriptive findings reveal that property owners are not currently motivated to pay their property taxes by the promise of collective benefits, but rather by the desire to secure their property rights. Thus, this study asks: What dimensions of property tax design do property owners prioritize? How do they negotiate trade-offs? And can policy design establish credible commitment? I address these questions using an experimental conjoint survey with over 2,000 property owners in 34 urban municipalities in Mexico. I find that property owners are largely indifferent to the progressivity of property tax increases yet strongly prioritize policies that enhance accountability in how government administers, spends, and enforces taxes. Specifically, the presence of active oversight strategies in the use of funds increases the support for a policy by 11 percentage points, an effect greater than that of policies that offer more direct benefits. Individuals with high perceptions of government corruption disproportionately favor tax policies that incorporate active transparency strategies even if this implies a higher fiscal burden. Overall, this study demonstrates that public support for property taxation is not entirely inelastic and that adopting accountability measures that signal how funds are spent can make property owners more accepting of tax increases. This study contributes to literature on the fiscal contract by demonstrating how policy designs that enhance accountability can help establish credible commitment.

中文摘要

财产税作为市政政府地方收入的主要来源，对于改善城市服务供给和居民福祉至关重要。然而，这一税种普遍不受欢迎，且常引发政治争议。本研究的描述性发现表明，业主目前并非因集体利益的承诺而缴纳财产税，而是出于保障自身产权的愿望。因此，本研究提出以下问题：业主优先关注财产税设计的哪些维度？他们如何权衡取舍？政策设计能否建立可信承诺？我通过在墨西哥34个城市市政区对超过2000名业主进行的实验性联合分析调查来解答这些问题。研究发现，业主对财产税增加的累进性基本不敏感，但强烈优先考虑增强政府在税收管理、支出和执行方面问责性的政策。具体而言，资金使用中积极监督策略的存在使政策支持率提高11个百分点，这一效果大于提供更直接利益的政策。对政府腐败感知较高的个体，即使这意味着更高的财政负担，也尤为倾向于支持包含积极透明策略的税收政策。总体而言，本研究证明公众对财产税的支持并非完全缺乏弹性，采取能表明资金使用方式的问责措施可使业主更易接受税收增加。本研究通过展示增强问责性的政策设计如何帮助建立可信承诺，为财政契约文献做出贡献。
