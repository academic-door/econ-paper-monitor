接受本地集中式动物饲养场开发的意愿：实际水质信息与农村居民补偿方式偏好

Willingness to accept local CAFO development: actual water quality information and rural residents’ preferences for means of compensation

Xinyue Liu, Li Zhou, Wuyang Hu, Xinqi Li

摘要

Concentrated animal feeding operations (CAFOs) provide low-cost products but often face local opposition due to environmental externalities. Based on a two-time panel discrete choice experiment (DCE) of rural residents living near CAFOs in China, this study quantifies how water quality information disclosure and various means of compensation affect residents’ acceptance of local CAFO development. The findings reveal that: (1) Compensation programmes incorporating quasi-public goods and environmental offsets can improve residents’ acceptance of CAFOs, with environmental offsets yielding the highest utility to respondents. (2) Water quality information disclosure heightens legitimate risk awareness, increasing the likelihood of opting out and rejecting CAFO construction. When the contribution of crop production to water pollution is clarified, residents receiving favourable water quality testing results show a higher willingness to accept CAFO development than those receiving unfavourable testing results. These results suggest that policymakers should prioritize environmental offsets, strengthen environmental regulation to ensure compliance, and provide the public with objective interpretations of risks. This study offers valuable insights for promoting sustainable CAFO development in China and economies with similar institutional contexts. 中文摘要

集约化畜禽养殖场（CAFOs）提供低成本产品，但常因环境外部性而面临当地居民的反对。基于对中国CAFOs周边农村居民开展的两期面板离散选择实验（DCE），本研究量化了水质信息披露及不同补偿方式对居民接受当地CAFOs开发意愿的影响。研究发现：（1）纳入准公共物品与环境补偿的补偿方案能够提升居民对CAFOs的接受度，其中环境补偿为受访者带来最高效用。（2）水质信息披露增强了居民的合法风险意识，提高了选择退出并拒绝CAFOs建设的可能性。当明确作物生产对水污染的贡献时，获得有利水质检测结果的居民比获得不利检测结果的居民表现出更高的CAFOs开发接受意愿。上述结果表明，政策制定者应优先考虑环境补偿，加强环境监管以确保合规，并向公众提供客观的风险解读。本研究为促进中国及具有相似制度背景的经济体中CAFOs的可持续发展提供了有价值的参考。关键词：集约化畜禽养殖场；信息披露；补偿方式；接受意愿 JEL分类：Q51 Q53 Q56 Q13
