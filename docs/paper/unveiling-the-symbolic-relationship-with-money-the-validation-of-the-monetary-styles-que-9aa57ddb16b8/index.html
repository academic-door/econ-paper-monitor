揭示与金钱的象征关系：货币风格问卷的验证

Unveiling the symbolic relationship with money: The validation of the monetary styles questionnaire

Edoardo Lozza, Giulia Sesini

摘要

The symbolic meanings of money play a critical role in people's lives, influencing economic practices, relationships, work motivation, and financial well-being. Despite a substantial literature in the psychology of money, no instrument has comprehensively measured the full range of symbolic meanings associated to money. This research aims to validate a novel instrument designed to investigate the symbolic dimensions of money, drawing upon a solid theoretical framework grounded in psychodynamic theory. The theory of Monetary Styles represents one of the first attempts to comprehensively account for the heterogeneity and diversity of money's symbolic significance. This framework distinguishes six monetary styles, patterns of relating to money that arise from its symbolic meanings and manifest in distinct behaviors and values. Based on this framework, the Monetary Styles Questionnaire was developed and validated across six studies. The findings showed psychometric robustness, validity over time, and reliability in both Italy and the U.S. Results further supported the theoretical framework by revealing meaningful associations between monetary styles and relevant constructs, including financial practices and work-related motivations. In addition to advancing theory and providing a new tool for measuring the symbolic meaning of money, this research offers significant implications for practitioners, educators, and policymakers seeking to foster healthier financial behaviors and economic well-being at both individual and societal levels.

中文摘要

金钱的象征意义在人们的生活中发挥着关键作用，影响着经济实践、人际关系、工作动机以及财务福祉。尽管金钱心理学已有大量文献，但尚无工具全面测量与金钱相关的象征意义之全部范围。本研究旨在验证一种新型测量工具，该工具基于以心理动力学理论为基础的坚实理论框架，用以考察金钱的象征维度。金钱风格理论是首批尝试全面解释金钱象征意义之异质性与多样性的理论之一。该框架区分了六种金钱风格，即由金钱象征意义所引发、并表现为不同行为与价值观的与金钱发生关联的模式。基于这一框架，研究者开发了金钱风格问卷，并通过六项研究对其进行了验证。研究结果表明该问卷具有良好的心理测量学稳健性、跨时间的效度，以及在意大利和美国两地的信度。结果还通过揭示金钱风格与相关构念（包括财务实践和工作相关动机）之间存在有意义的关联，进一步支持了该理论框架。除了推进理论并为测量金钱的象征意义提供新工具外，本研究还为致力于在个体与社会层面促进更健康的财务行为和经济效益的从业者、教育者和政策制定者提供了重要启示。
