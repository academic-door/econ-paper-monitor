Open public data and stock price delay: evidence from a quasi-natural experiment in China

摘要

Stock price delay serves as a key metric of information efficiency in stock markets, and identifying its determinants is essential for promoting market information efficiency. This study examines the effect of open public data (OPD) on stock price delay. Using the staggered adoption of city-level OPD portals in China as a quasi-natural experiment, our difference-in-differences analysis finds that OPD significantly reduces stock price delay, with an economic magnitude representing approximately 7.9% of its standard deviation. Cross-sectional analyses demonstrate that this effect is more pronounced when (1) public data on OPD portals are of higher quality, (2) public data are more relevant to firm operations, (3) public data are less substitutable, (4) investors have greater information processing capacity, and (5) firms’ information disclosure quality is lower. Additional tests show that OPD implementation is associated with improved liquidity, reduced return volatility, and diminished information content of earnings announcements. This study makes a valuable contribution to the literature on the determinants of stock price delay and the economic consequences of OPD, while offering insights for policymakers seeking to enhance market information efficiency and assess the effectiveness of OPD initiatives. 1 Under the Chinese institutional setting, a firm’s registered city generally coincides with the location of its headquarters rather than merely serving as a legal place of incorporation. According to the Company Law of the People’s Republic of China and the Regulations on the Administration of Market Entities Registration , a company’s registered domicile must correspond to the location of its principal administrative office, and firms relocating their principal office are required to update their registration accordingly (Chen, Hung, and Wang Citation 2018). Consequently, firms’ registered cities generally represent the locations of their headquarters. Because local governments in China play an active role in firms’ operations and are responsible for taxation, administrative approvals, industrial policy implementation, regulatory oversight, and other aspects of the local business environment (Xu Citation 2011), firms’ registered cities also represent the local institutional and information environments surrounding their headquarters. 3 GF Securities has developed an enterprise financial intelligent early-warning platform based on large-scale pre-trained models, which leverages public data and artificial intelligence to identify corporate financial risks at earlier stages and with greater accuracy. Similarly, the Shenzhen Stock Exchange’s blockchain-based industry risk data sharing project enhances the secure sharing of risk information across market participants, enabling investors to better assess industry-level risks. The Shanghai Equity Exchange has introduced a blockchain-based equity registration, custody, and transfer service platform that integrates multi-source data and provides more objective valuation benchmarks for investors in equity markets. 中文摘要

股票价格延迟是衡量股票市场信息效率的关键指标，识别其决定因素对于促进市场信息效率至关重要。本研究考察了开放公共数据（OPD）对股票价格延迟的影响。利用中国城市级OPD门户网站的分阶段采纳作为准自然实验，我们的双重差分分析发现，OPD显著降低了股票价格延迟，其经济幅度约占其标准差7.9%。横截面分析表明，当（1）OPD门户网站上的公共数据质量更高，（2）公共数据与企业运营更相关，（3）公共数据的可替代性较低，（4）投资者的信息处理能力更强，以及（5）企业的信息披露质量较低时，这一效应更为显著。附加测试显示，OPD的实施与流动性改善、回报波动性降低以及盈余公告信息含量减少相关联。本研究为股票价格延迟决定因素及OPD经济后果的文献做出了有价值的贡献，同时为旨在提升市场信息效率和评估OPD举措有效性的政策制定者提供了见解。¹ 在中国制度背景下，企业的注册城市通常与其总部所在地一致，而非仅仅作为法定注册地。根据《中华人民共和国公司法》和《市场主体登记管理条例》，公司的注册住所必须与其主要办事机构所在地相符，企业迁移主要办事机构时需相应更新注册信息（Chen, Hung, and Wang Citation 2018）。因此，企业的注册城市通常代表其总部所在地。由于中国地方政府在企业运营中发挥积极作用，并负责税收、行政审批、产业政策实施、监管监督以及地方商业环境其他方面的事务（Xu Citation 2011），企业的注册城市也代表了其总部周边的本地制度和信息环境。³ 广发证券基于大规模预训练模型开发了企业财务智能预警平台，该平台利用公共数据和人工智能，以更早阶段和更高准确性识别企业财务风险。类似地，深圳证券交易所基于区块链的行业风险数据共享项目增强了市场参与者之间风险信息的安全共享，使投资者能够更好地评估行业层面风险。上海股权交易所推出了基于区块链的股权登记、托管和转让服务平台，该平台整合多源数据，为股权市场投资者提供更客观的估值基准。更多详情，请参见 https://www.csrc.gov.cn/shanxi/c106408/c7568249/content.shtml、https://jrj.sh.gov.cn/SC212/20241023/7f10aa43b0424212b06e0385adaece26.html 和 https://finance.stockstar.com/IG2024102200029365.shtml。⁴ 在本研究中，由于样本仅限于中国A股市场，企业-日观测值等同于证券-日观测值。方程Eq.
