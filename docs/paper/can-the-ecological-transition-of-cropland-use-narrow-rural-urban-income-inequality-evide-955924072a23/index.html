耕地利用生态转型能否缩小城乡收入差距？——来自中国黄淮海地区的证据

Can the ecological transition of cropland use narrow rural-urban income inequality? Evidence from China’s Huang-Huai-Hai Region

Linpeng Yang, Yansui Liu, Cong Ou, Yurui Li

摘要

Rural-urban income inequality (RUIE) remains a persistent challenge across many rapidly developing regions, undermining socio-economic welfare and hindering sustainable development. The ecological transition of cropland use (ETCU), a management paradigm rooted in the principles of sustainable intensification, is widely advocated to enhance agricultural sustainability. While localized case studies suggest that such practices can bolster rural incomes through improved resource efficiency or product diversification, robust empirical evidence on whether large‑scale ETCU adoption mitigates regional RUIE remains scarce, and the underlying systemic mechanisms are poorly understood. This study develops a multi-scale "cropland-agriculture-rural" framework to analyze how ETCU influences RUIE in China's intensively cultivated Huang-Huai-Hai Region. By integrating plot-scale biogeochemical modeling (PEST-DNDC), county-level panel data (2000–2020), and Structural Equation Modeling, we systematically quantify the spatiotemporal patterns and causal pathways of this transition. The results demonstrate that: (1) ETCU and RUIE exhibit spatially heterogeneous co-evolution; improvements in ETCU are not consistently associated with reduced inequality, and in some coastal counties, they even coincide with widening gaps. (2) ETCU affects RUIE primarily by restructuring agricultural multifunctionality, which serves as the core mediator. (3) The effects are highly dimension-specific. Enhancing resource-use efficiency exerts a modest yet significant direct mitigating effect on RUIE. Conversely, improvements in productivity and environmental performance exhibit no direct effect; instead, they indirectly exacerbate inequality by weakening the agricultural product supply function—which our model identifies as a strong and direct suppressor of RUIE. The economic value-added and ecological conservation functions do not exhibit significant direct equalizing effects. These findings indicate that the equity outcomes of ETCU are not automatic but depend critically on how it reshapes the multifunctional structure of agriculture. Policy interventions must therefore adopt an integrated approach that strategically guides the agricultural transition toward balanced multifunctionality, safeguards diversified production, and ensures that efficiency gains translate into broadly shared benefits for both rural and urban households.

中文摘要

城乡收入不平等（RUIE）在许多快速发展的地区依然是一个持续存在的挑战，削弱了社会经济福祉并阻碍了可持续发展。耕地利用生态转型（ETCU）作为一种根植于可持续集约化原则的管理范式，被广泛倡导以提升农业可持续性。尽管局部案例研究表明，此类实践可通过提高资源效率或产品多样化来增加农村收入，但关于大规模ETCU采纳是否缓解区域RUIE的稳健实证证据仍然稀缺，其潜在的系统性机制也尚不明确。本研究构建了一个多尺度“耕地—农业—农村”框架，以分析ETCU如何影响中国集约化耕作的黄淮海地区的RUIE。通过整合地块尺度生物地球化学模型（PEST-DNDC）、县级面板数据（2000—2020年）以及结构方程模型，我们系统量化了这一转型的时空格局与因果路径。结果表明：（1）ETCU与RUIE呈现出空间异质的协同演化；ETCU的改善并非始终与不平等缩小相关联，在某些沿海县甚至伴随着差距扩大。（2）ETCU主要通过重构农业多功能性来影响RUIE，后者构成核心中介变量。（3）其效应具有高度维度特异性。资源利用效率的提升对RUIE产生适度但显著的直接缓解作用。相反，生产力与环境绩效的改善并无直接效应；而是通过削弱农产品供给功能间接加剧不平等——我们的模型识别出该功能是RUIE的强直接抑制因素。经济附加值功能与生态保育功能未表现出显著的直接均等化效应。这些发现表明，ETCU的公平结果并非自动实现，而是关键取决于其如何重塑农业的多功能结构。因此，政策干预必须采取综合路径，战略性地引导农业转型走向均衡的多功能性，保障多样化生产，并确保效率提升转化为城乡家庭广泛共享的福祉。
