农业经济学会百年

A Hundred Years of the Agricultural Economics Society

David R. Stead

农业与食品发展经济学宏观与货币国际贸易

摘要

The centenary of the UK‐based Agricultural Economics Society (AES) is an obvious opportunity to provide an updated summary institutional history of its development and achievements. Knowledge of equivalent associations in the US influenced the foundation of the AES in 1926. After a faltering start, the Society's main activities have been organising residential and one‐day conferences and publishing the Journal of Agricultural Economics . The last‐mentioned began in 1928 as a conference proceedings. Great growth in AES membership occurred in the 25 years after the Second World War, reflecting recognition of the importance of agricultural economics in that era. With the subsequent erosion of the subject's base in British universities, especially around the turn of the millennium, the twenty‐first‐century AES pursued an outward‐looking strategy. It started co‐producing the outreach periodical EuroChoices with the European Association of Agricultural Economists, and holding annual conferences outside Britain. Nowadays AES membership is much more international than in the past but less occupationally diverse, with around three‐quarters of current members drawn from academia. When deemed necessary the Society has occasionally made representations on behalf of the profession, in particular influencing specific aspects of how UK research assessment exercises operate. Numbers of members and annual conference delegates are currently buoyant, and the Executive Committee has launched a £100,000 centenary fund to support postgraduate research students. The AES, then, is well positioned for the opportunities and challenges ahead at the start of its second century.

中文摘要

英国农业经济学会（AES）百年华诞，为更新其发展历程与成就的制度史摘要提供了良机。对美国同类学会的了解影响了1926年AES的创立。在起步蹒跚之后，学会的主要活动一直是举办住宿式和单日会议，并出版《农业经济学杂志》。该杂志始于1928年，最初为会议论文集。二战后25年间，AES会员数量大幅增长，反映出当时人们对农业经济学重要性的认可。随着该学科在英国大学中的基础随后遭到侵蚀，尤其是在千年之交前后，21世纪的AES推行了外向型战略。它开始与欧洲农业经济学家协会共同创办外联期刊《EuroChoices》，并在英国以外举行年会。如今，AES会员远比过去国际化，但职业构成多样性下降，目前约四分之三的会员来自学术界。在认为必要时，学会偶尔代表本专业发声，尤其影响了英国研究评估活动的具体运作方式。目前会员人数和年会代表人数均呈上升态势，执行委员会已设立一项10万英镑的百年基金，用于支持研究生。因此，在第二个百年伊始，AES已为未来的机遇与挑战做好了充分准备。
