信息呈现方式对消费者实验中内部与外部效度的影响

Implications of information presentation mode for internal and external validity in consumer experiments

Christopher R. Gustafson, Antoine Champetier

摘要

The causal impact of information on consumer decision-making has been a prime area of interest for issues including novel food technologies, nutrition, food safety, and food authenticity. Experiments are oftentimes the only way to estimate causal impacts of information, as secondary data lack details on individual-specific information exposure. Response to information is relevant for many policy questions and has been formally integrated into some policy processes, such as those related to provision of nutrition information. However, the standard approach, which exposes participants to information, may provide inaccurate predictions of real-world outcomes. In the real-world, consumers may choose whether to access information, driven by a variety of motives. Forced exposure in experiments may also unintentionally bias results—threatening the internal validity of the design—by signaling the information’s importance to participants. This study investigates how different methods of presenting information impact both the internal and external validity of research designs. Participants were randomized to one of four conditions: information exposure, opt out to bypass information, opt in to access information, and a no-information control. Our findings indicate that the information presentation method has large impacts on external validity, demonstrated by significant differences in results across conditions. Information provision mode also yields significant, though smaller, impacts on internal validity among the subset of participants who accessed the information. In the study, participants are exposed to information about honey fraud, a topic that is both present in general media and of concern for the beekeeping industry globally.

中文摘要

信息对消费者决策的因果影响一直是新型食品技术、营养、食品安全及食品真实性等议题中的核心研究领域。由于二手数据缺乏个体特定信息暴露的细节，实验往往成为估计信息因果影响的唯一途径。对信息的反应与众多政策问题相关，并已被正式纳入某些政策流程，例如与营养信息提供相关的政策。然而，标准方法——即让参与者暴露于信息——可能对现实世界结果的预测不够准确。在现实情境中，消费者可能出于多种动机选择是否获取信息。实验中的强制暴露也可能通过向参与者暗示信息的重要性，无意中引入偏差，从而威胁设计的内在有效性。本研究探讨了不同信息呈现方式如何影响研究设计的内在和外在有效性。参与者被随机分配到四种条件之一：信息暴露、选择退出以绕过信息、选择加入以获取信息，以及无信息对照组。我们的研究结果表明，信息呈现方式对外在有效性有显著影响，各条件间结果存在显著差异。信息提供模式对获取信息的参与者子集的内在有效性也产生了显著但较小的影响。在本研究中，参与者接触到的信息涉及蜂蜜欺诈，这一话题既常见于大众媒体，也是全球养蜂业关注的焦点。
