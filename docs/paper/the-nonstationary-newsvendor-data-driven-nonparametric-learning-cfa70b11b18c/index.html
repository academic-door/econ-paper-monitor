非平稳报童问题：数据驱动的非参数学习

The Nonstationary Newsvendor: Data-Driven Nonparametric Learning

N. Bora Keskin, Xu Min, Jing-Sheng Song

摘要

We study a newsvendor problem with unknown demand distribution in a nonstationary demand environment over a multiperiod time horizon. The demand in each period consists of a time-varying demand level and an additive random shock. Neither the demand level nor the random shock is separately observable. The amount of change in the demand level over the time horizon is measured by a cumulative variation metric. The problem has widespread applications, such as perishable inventory planning, staffing, and medical resource capacity planning in the wake of COVID-19. We design a family of nonparametric dynamic ordering policies, termed two-stage estimation (2SE) policies, that track the shifts in the unknown demand level while accounting for the unobservable random demand shocks. To compute the order quantity in each period, these policies only need the past demand observations, without any access to the underlying demand distribution. For a finite variation “budget,” we prove that our ordering policies are first-order optimal in the sense that their regret grows at the smallest possible rate. We also extend our analysis to the case of asymptotically large variation budgets. Through case studies based on real-life data, we show that our policies can save more than 20% of overage and underage costs, relative to policies widely used for perishable inventory replenishment and nurse staffing. Moreover, our simulation experiments indicate that our policies consistently maintain superior performance across diverse patterns of nonstationary demand environments. 中文摘要

我们研究了一个在非平稳需求环境下，多期时间范围内需求分布未知的报童问题。每期的需求由时变的需求水平与加性随机冲击构成，而需求水平与随机冲击均无法单独观测。需求水平随时间变化的幅度通过累积变化度量来衡量。该问题具有广泛的应用背景，如易腐品库存规划、人员配置以及新冠疫情背景下的医疗资源容量规划。我们设计了一族非参数动态订货策略，称为两阶段估计策略，该策略在考虑不可观测的随机需求冲击的同时，追踪未知需求水平的变化。这些策略在计算每期订货量时，仅需历史需求观测数据，无需接触潜在的需求分布。对于有限的变化“预算”，我们证明了所提出的订货策略在遗憾值以最小可能速率增长的意义下是一阶最优的。我们还将分析扩展至渐近大变化预算的情形。基于真实数据的案例研究表明，与广泛用于易腐品库存补货和护士配置的策略相比，我们的策略能节省超过20%的超储与缺货成本。此外，模拟实验表明，我们的策略在多样化的非平稳需求环境模式下始终保持优越性能。本文由David Simchi-Levi负责运营管理方向审阅。资金支持：N. B. Keskin和J.-S. Song获得了杜克大学富卡商学院的支持，X. Min获得了国家自然科学基金[批准号72401195]和中国博士后科学基金[2025T180216]的资助。补充材料：在线附录和数据文件可在https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2023.01823获取。
