衡量有用知识文化及创新根源

Measuring the Culture of Useful Knowledge and the Roots of Innovation

Marc Klemp

摘要

I develop a method that uses the language surrounding places, people, and other entities in dated text to measure their attributes over time, and use it to ask whether a local orientation toward useful knowledge predicts innovation and growth. Patterns of word use form a numerical map, or word embedding, in which fixed word lists define attribute scales for scoring each entity. Neither scales nor scores use outcomes, and publication dates restrict scores to prior text. Applied to 38.6 billion context-word occurrences in nearly 2.7 million English-language works from 1500-1899, the method recovers independently measured elevation and agricultural dependence and combines learning, science, practical improvement, and print in a useful-knowledge index. Within countries, a one-standard-deviation increase in the index is associated with 0.105 standard deviations higher log(1+patents) during 1980-2014 and a 3.4 percentage-point increase in the probability of any patent. The association remains 0.062 standard deviations in 7,011 cells without a recorded birthplace of a notable intellectual. An index reconstructed from pre-1750 contexts predicts 1980-2014 log patenting within countries at 0.053 standard deviations after controlling for textual coverage, with positive estimates in Europe, Asia, and Latin America. Training the embedding solely on pre-1750 text leaves the full-sample association positive. Within countries, the 1500-1899 index also predicts average nighttime light over 1992-2024 and regional GDP per capita in each region's latest available year, 2016-2020. In European cities, the pre-1750 index predicts faster post-1750 growth near coal, a complementarity concentrated in Britain. The findings support Mokyr's view of useful knowledge as a local innovative capacity and an Allen-Mokyr synthesis for Britain's coal-and-steam take-off.

中文摘要

我提出一种方法，利用文本中围绕地点、人物及其他实体的语言，测量其随时间变化的属性，并以此探究对实用知识的地方性取向能否预测创新与增长。词汇使用模式构成数值化地图（即词嵌入），其中固定词表定义属性标尺，用于对每个实体进行评分。标尺与评分均不依赖结果变量，且出版日期将评分限定于历史文本。将该方法应用于1500-1899年间近270万部英文著作中的386亿个上下文词汇实例后，该方法成功还原了独立测量的海拔高度与农业依赖度，并将学习、科学、实践改进与印刷术整合为实用知识指数。在国家层面，该指数每增加一个标准差，1980-2014年间log(1+专利数)提高0.105个标准差，且产生任何专利的概率上升3.4个百分点。在7011个未记录著名知识分子出生地的单元格中，该关联仍保持0.062个标准差。基于1750年前文本重建的指数，在控制文本覆盖范围后，可预测1980-2014年国家内log专利数达0.053个标准差，且欧洲、亚洲与拉丁美洲均呈现正向估计值。仅以1750年前文本训练词嵌入后，全样本关联仍保持正向。在国家层面，1500-1899年指数还可预测1992-2024年平均夜间灯光强度，以及各地区最新可用年份（2016-2020年）的区域人均GDP。在欧洲城市中，1750年前指数可预测1750年后煤炭资源附近更快的增长，这种互补性集中体现在英国。研究结果支持Mokyr关于实用知识作为地方创新能力的观点，以及英国煤炭与蒸汽腾飞的Allen-Mokyr综合解释。
