加纳的性别、土地权利与气候变化适应：对土地权属、农业和农村发展的影响

Gender, land rights and climate change adaptation in Ghana: Implications for land tenure, agriculture and rural development

Peter Asare‐Nuamah, David Anaafo, Tina Beuchelt

摘要

Land rights, important dimensions of land tenure, are crucial for smallholder farmers’ land tenure security and climate change adaptation. However, there are knowledge gaps on the nexus between land rights and climate adaptation in Ghana, particularly from a gendered perspective. This study draws on land right frameworks to explore the nexus between gender, land rights and climate change adaptation among men and women smallholder farmers in rural Ghana. Data were collected through a randomized survey administered to 510 smallholder farmers from four purposively selected communities in the Adansi Asokwa District, and analyzed using descriptive and inferential statistics. The results showed that compared to women, more men own more farmlands (averagely 6.8 vs. 5.4 acres for women), have formal land title deeds, and exercise more land rights, particularly exclusion and alienation rights, enhancing their tenure security. Besides frequency of pest invasion, there is no significant differences in men’s and women’s climate change perceptions and experiences. Farmers used multiple climate adaptation strategies, including planting improved crop varieties, changing planting season, climate information services and agrochemical application. We found a significant difference between men’s and women’s adoption of crop diversification, crop-livestock integration and seed storage. Unlike men’s adaptation strategies, those of women are influenced by the number of rights they exercise and farmland size. Also, perceptions about the raining season and temperature intensity as well as marital status, locality, years of farming experience, access to non-farm livelihood activities and credit significantly influence farmers’ climate change adaptation. Gender differentiated policies and incentives for farmers are needed to enhance tenure security and climate change adaptation.

中文摘要

土地权利作为土地制度的重要维度，对小农户的土地权属保障和气候变化适应能力至关重要。然而，关于加纳土地权利与气候适应之间关系的研究仍存在知识空白，尤其是从性别视角切入的分析。本研究基于土地权利理论框架，探讨加纳农村地区男女小农户在性别、土地权利与气候变化适应之间的关联机制。通过对阿丹西阿索夸地区四个定向选取社区的510名小农户进行随机问卷调查，并运用描述性统计与推断性统计分析数据。结果表明：相较于女性，男性拥有更多农田（平均6.8英亩 vs 女性5.4英亩）、持有正式土地所有权证书，并享有更多土地权利（特别是排他权和转让权），从而增强了其土地权属保障。除虫害侵袭频率外，男女农户在气候变化认知与经历方面无显著差异。农户采用多种气候适应策略，包括种植改良作物品种、调整种植季节、利用气候信息服务及施用农用化学品。研究发现，男女农户在作物多样化、种养结合及种子储存方面的采纳行为存在显著差异。与男性适应策略不同，女性农户的适应行为受其行使权利数量及农田面积的影响。此外，对雨季和气温强度的认知、婚姻状况、居住地、务农年限、非农生计活动参与及信贷获取等因素显著影响农户的气候变化适应能力。需制定性别差异化的农户政策与激励措施，以增强土地权属保障和气候变化适应能力。
