英国新闻媒体中超加工食品的框架（2022—2023年）及其与引述行动者利益的关联：一项混合方法研究

Framing of ultra-processed foods in UK news media (2022–2023) and associations with quoted actors’ interests: a mixed-methods study

Jennie C Parnham, Kiara Chang, Michael Essman, Deb Smith, Mike P Etkind 等

摘要

Increasing attention on “ultra-processed” foods (UPFs) and their health effects has sparked policy debate. News media influences public perceptions yet understanding of UPF framing and industry influence remains limited. This study analysed UPF framing in UK news media and trade press, exploring interests of quoted individuals and organisations. We systematically searched UK media for UPF articles in 2022–23, identifying 188 articles for mixed-methods content analysis. Articles were examined for their position towards classifying food by processing-level (accepted/contested/mixed). Framing Theory guided identification of UPF frames describing problem definitions, causes, and solutions. Actors’ interests were classified using academic journal guidelines. Half the articles (52%) accepted the UPF concept, 19% contested it, and 29% were mixed. Five frames were identified. The ‘Social Justice’, ‘Lifestyle Drift’, and ‘Science Report’ frames accepted the UPF concept, with the ‘Social Justice’ frame positioning UPF consumption as a structural public health problem requiring policy action such as taxes or front-of-package labelling. The ‘UPF Critique’ and ‘Market Justice’ frames contested the scientific basis of the UPF concept, proposing nutrient-based approaches instead. Among 116 named actors, 33% had UPF industry interests, with academics comprising 45% and industry spokespeople 11%. Actors with UPF industry interests were more commonly cited in contesting frames (e.g. ‘Market Justice’: 73%) than accepting frames (e.g. ‘Social Justice’: 10%). These findings highlight that industry influence on UPF media discourse is often obscured behind academic voices. Greater transparency around actor interests is urgently needed to ensure emerging UPF policies are shaped by independent evidence.

中文摘要

关于“超加工食品”（UPFs）及其健康影响的关注日益增加，引发了政策辩论。新闻媒体影响公众认知，但对UPF框架构建及行业影响力的理解仍然有限。本研究分析了英国新闻媒体和行业出版物中UPF的框架构建，探讨了被引用的个人和组织的利益关系。我们系统检索了2022-23年间英国媒体中关于UPF的文章，识别出188篇文章进行混合方法内容分析。文章根据其对按加工水平分类食品的立场（接受/质疑/混合）进行审查。框架理论指导了描述问题定义、原因和解决方案的UPF框架识别。行动者的利益根据学术期刊指南进行分类。半数文章（52%）接受UPF概念，19%质疑，29%持混合态度。识别出五种框架。“社会正义”、“生活方式漂移”和“科学报告”框架接受UPF概念，其中“社会正义”框架将UPF消费定位为结构性公共卫生问题，需要税收或前包装标签等政策行动。“UPF批判”和“市场正义”框架质疑UPF概念的科学基础，提出基于营养的方法作为替代。在116名具名行动者中，33%具有UPF行业利益，学者占45%，行业发言人占11%。具有UPF行业利益的行动者更常见于质疑框架（如“市场正义”：73%）而非接受框架（如“社会正义”：10%）。这些发现强调，行业对UPF媒体话语的影响常常隐藏在学术声音背后。迫切需要提高行动者利益的透明度，以确保新兴的UPF政策由独立证据塑造。
