家庭通胀预期调查中的无应答偏差

Nonresponse Bias in Household Inflation Expectations Surveys

MELTEM CHADWICK, RENNAE CHERRY, JAQUESON K. GALIMBERTI

摘要

This paper uses microdata from the Reserve Bank of New Zealand's Household Inflation Expectations survey to evaluate the effects of nonresponses to the inflation expectations question in the survey. We find nonresponses lead to substantial underrepresentation of some demographic groups in the survey: young, female, low‐income, and minority ethnic groups have lower response rates. How the survey is conducted also affects item response rates. The survey response rates increase when the survey is conducted online and when inflation rates deviate from the central bank's target range. Using a sample selection model, we assess whether the survey has item nonresponse bias by comparing the demographic characteristics of responders and nonresponders. After accounting for selection, we find that observed differences in inflation expectations by gender, ethnicity, and income decrease substantially, while differences by age increase. We quantify and demonstrate how to adjust average aggregate and subgroup inflation expectations for bias caused by item nonresponse. We show that there is a positive bias, and the aggregate and subgroup inflation expectation series shift down after the adjustment. We also show that inflation expectations disagreement, both across and within subgroups, tends to decrease with the correction for nonresponse bias. These findings have important implications for survey design and monetary policy communication.

中文摘要

本文利用新西兰储备银行家庭通胀预期调查的微观数据，评估调查中通胀预期问题未答复的影响。我们发现，未答复导致调查中某些人口群体代表性严重不足：年轻、女性、低收入及少数族裔群体的答复率较低。调查实施方式也影响项目答复率。当调查在线进行且通胀率偏离中央银行目标区间时，调查答复率上升。利用样本选择模型，我们通过比较答复者与未答复者的人口特征，评估调查是否存在项目未答复偏差。在考虑选择效应后，我们发现按性别、族裔和收入划分的通胀预期观测差异显著缩小，而按年龄划分的差异则增大。我们量化并展示了如何调整平均总体及子群体通胀预期以纠正项目未答复造成的偏差。结果表明存在正向偏差，调整后总体及子群体通胀预期序列下移。我们还发现，通胀预期分歧，无论是子群体内部还是子群体之间，在纠正未答复偏差后趋于减小。这些发现对调查设计和货币政策沟通具有重要意义。
