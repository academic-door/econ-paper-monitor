当损耗可预测时：机器学习与企业杠杆动态的估计

When attrition is predictable: machine learning and the estimation of corporate leverage dynamics

Nargis Sultana, Fahad Zeya

摘要

Firm-level data sets suffer from panel attrition, and it can also be a problem with dynamic panel estimates. In this paper, we explore the question of whether the leverage estimates of a firm are meaningfully impacted by a predictable exit event, after adjusting for firm and time characteristics. We use a cross-fitted machine-learning model to estimate survival probabilities for an unbalanced panel of firms from 1999 to 2024, using lagged financial characteristics. Predictions show almost perfect separation between the groups of firms that survive and the group of firms that exit, suggesting little common support. We build stabilized inverse probability weights and re-estimate dynamic leverage models with firm and year fixed-effects. The weighted estimates suggest there is less leverage persistence and more speed of adjustment to the target leverage than conventional fixed-effects estimates, and the core qualitative relationships are largely unchanged. Therefore, the direction of estimated leverage dynamics is not really affected by predictable attrition, as it is the magnitude that is affected. The weighted estimates are only interpreted as sensitivity evidence due to limited overlap; there are no globally identified causal effects. 中文摘要

企业层面的数据集常面临面板 attrition（样本流失）问题，这一问题同样可能影响动态面板估计。本文探讨在调整企业与时间特征后，企业杠杆估计是否受到可预测退出事件的显著影响。我们采用交叉拟合机器学习模型，基于滞后财务特征，对1999年至2024年间非平衡面板企业样本的生存概率进行估计。预测结果显示，存活企业与退出企业群体之间几乎完全分离，表明共同支持域极为有限。我们构建了稳定化逆概率权重，并重新估计了包含企业与年份固定效应的动态杠杆模型。加权估计结果表明，相较于传统固定效应估计，杠杆持续性有所降低，而向目标杠杆调整的速度则加快，核心定性关系基本保持不变。因此，可预测 attrition 对估计的杠杆动态方向影响不大，主要影响的是其幅度。由于共同支持域有限，加权估计仅作为敏感性证据解读，不存在全局识别的因果效应。关键词：Attrition 偏差；企业杠杆；机器学习；面板数据；选择调整 JEL分类号：C23 C55 G32 致谢：我们衷心感谢编辑 Mark Taylor 教授在审稿过程中给予的指导与建设性反馈。同时，我们也感谢两位匿名审稿人的细致评估及深刻且富有建设性的意见，这些意见显著提升了我们稿件的质量、清晰度与严谨性。
