地方政府中的种族与选区划分

Race and Redistricting in Local Governments

Carlos Fernando Avenancio-León, Daniel B. Jones, Ying Shi

公共与政治经济学城市与区域不平等

摘要

Electoral maps translate residential geography into political representation, yet they have never been systematically measured for thousands of local governments across the country. We reconstruct 3,712 city council and county commission maps across 18,521 districts using nationwide voter-file and Census data. Enacted maps overrepresent White voters and underrepresent Black and Hispanic voters, with gaps predicting the racial composition of officeholders. We juxtapose these with simulated, race-blind maps satisfying common districting constraints. Simulated maps reproduce much of this disparity, showing that geography skews representation before any boundary-drawing. Enacted maps substantially improve on the race-blind baseline only in jurisdictions subject to oversight from the Voting Rights Act or districting litigation. As courts curtail race-conscious districting, local governments may revert to a baseline that is formally neutral but substantively unequal.

中文摘要

选举地图将居住地理转化为政治代表权，然而，在全国数千个地方政府中，这些地图从未被系统性地衡量过。我们利用全国选民档案和人口普查数据，重建了覆盖18,521个选区的3,712个市议会和县委员会地图。已实施的地图过度代表了白人选民，而低估了黑人和西班牙裔选民，这些差距预示着公职人员的种族构成。我们将这些地图与满足常见选区划分约束的模拟、种族盲视地图进行对比。模拟地图再现了大部分这种差异，表明在任何边界划定之前，地理就已经扭曲了代表权。只有在受《投票权法案》或选区划分诉讼监督的司法管辖区，已实施的地图才显著优于种族盲视基线。随着法院限制种族意识选区划分，地方政府可能回归到一种形式上中立但实质上不平等的基线状态。
