蓝色与绿色是天然盟友吗？绘制全球公正转型的公众基础

Are the blue and the green natural allies? Mapping the public foundations of just transition worldwide

Mengqi Xie, Sijeong Lim

摘要

A Just Transition, with its dual goals of environmental sustainability and social equity, has gained global prominence and has been adopted as a formal policy agenda by governments and intergovernmental organizations. While broad and meaningful public support for both goals is crucial to effective implementation, research on public attitudes has largely focused on developed Western countries or specific vulnerable populations. To address this gap, we examine whether and how support for environmental protection aligns or conflicts with backing for progressive redistribution across a diverse range of developed and developing countries. We posit that access to economic safety nets enables broader alignment at the population level (i.e., breadth of support), while post-materialist values and lived endorsement of the welfare state foster stronger individual-level alignment (i.e., depth of support). Our analysis, covering 95 countries from 2005 to 2022, finds support for these expectations. First, the population share of Green-Blue progressives supporting both goals is notably smaller in developing countries with high economic insecurity. Second, one’s environmental and redistributive attitudes in much of the Global South are unaligned or negatively correlated, in contrast to the positive individual-level alignment observed in developed countries, particularly among post-materialists and in generous welfare states. Regional variation is also significant: Europe ranks high in breadth and shows significant depth; North America shows strong depth but no significant breadth; East Asia demonstrates wide breadth but comparatively shallow depth. Sub-Saharan Africa lags significantly on both dimensions. This global variation calls for context-sensitive Just Transition policies that account for distinct socioeconomic realities.

中文摘要

公正转型兼具环境可持续性与社会公平的双重目标，已在全球范围内获得显著关注，并被各国政府和政府间组织采纳为正式政策议程。尽管公众对这两项目标的广泛而实质性的支持对于有效实施至关重要，但关于公众态度的研究在很大程度上集中于西方发达国家或特定弱势群体。为填补这一空白，我们考察在多个发达与发展中国家中，对环境保护的支持与对进步性再分配的支持之间是否以及如何一致或冲突。我们假设，经济安全网的可得性能够在人口层面促成更广泛的一致性（即支持的广度），而后物质主义价值观和对福利国家的切身认同则促进更强的个体层面一致性（即支持的深度）。我们的分析涵盖2005年至2022年的95个国家，结果支持了这些预期。第一，在经济不安全感较高的发展中国家，同时支持两项目标的“绿蓝进步派”人口比例明显较小。第二，在全球南方许多地区，个人的环境态度与再分配态度并不一致或呈负相关，这与发达国家观察到的个体层面正相关形成对比，尤其是在后物质主义者群体和福利慷慨的国家中。区域差异也十分显著：欧洲在广度上排名较高，并表现出显著的深度；北美表现出较强的深度，但广度不显著；东亚展现出广泛的广度，但深度相对较浅。撒哈拉以南非洲在两个维度上均明显落后。这种全球差异要求制定情境敏感的公正转型政策，以考虑不同的社会经济现实。
