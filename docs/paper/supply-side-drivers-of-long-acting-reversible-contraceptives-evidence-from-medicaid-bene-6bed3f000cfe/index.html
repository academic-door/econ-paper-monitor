Supply-Side Drivers of Long-Acting Reversible Contraceptives: Evidence from Medicaid Beneficiaries

摘要

U.S. federal law mandates that most insurers offer contraceptives for free to patients, yet nearly one in three women of reproductive age still report inadequate access to contraceptive services. With demand-side affordability barriers largely removed, supply-side factors—the actions of healthcare facilities and providers—may be critical in explaining this unmet need. This paper examines supply-side determinants of long-acting reversible contraceptive (LARC) use among Medicaid beneficiaries, a population for whom both LARCs and oral contraceptives (OCs) are covered at zero out-of-pocket cost. Unlike OCs, LARCs require clinician insertion and upfront device stocking, making them especially vulnerable to supply-side forces. Using national Medicaid claims data from 2015–2019 and a movers design that separates place-based from patient-based variation, we find that local supply-side factors explain 58.5 percent of geographic variation in LARC use—substantially more than the 37.7 percent explained for OCs. We then estimate the causal effects of a supply-side policy that substantially lowers the cost of stocking LARCs for safety-net providers: the 340B Drug Pricing Program. Leveraging the staggered timing of program participation across clinics in an event-study design, we find that the arrival of the first participating clinic in an enrollee's ZIP code increases LARC use by 4.3 percent without displacing OC use or increasing LARC removals. Together, these findings identify the supply side as both a key source of unmet contraceptive need and a promising target for policy to improve access to the most effective forms of contraception.

中文摘要

美国联邦法律规定大多数保险公司必须向患者免费提供避孕药具，但近三分之一育龄妇女仍报告避孕服务获取不足。在需求侧可负担性障碍基本消除的情况下，供给侧因素——医疗机构和提供者的行为——可能是解释这一未满足需求的关键。本文考察医疗补助受益人中长效可逆避孕药具（LARC）使用的供给侧决定因素，该人群的LARC和口服避孕药（OC）均以零自付费用覆盖。与OC不同，LARC需要临床医生置入和预先设备储备，使其特别容易受到供给侧力量的影响。使用2015—2019年全国医疗补助索赔数据以及将地点变异与患者变异分离的迁移者设计，我们发现当地供给侧因素解释了LARC使用地理变异的58.5%——大幅高于OC的37.7%。然后我们估计了一项大幅降低安全网提供者储备LARC成本的供给侧政策的因果效应：340B药品定价计划。利用事件研究设计中各诊所参与计划时间的交错性，我们发现参保人邮政编码区域内首家参与诊所的出现使LARC使用增加4.3%，且未取代OC使用或增加LARC取出。总之，这些发现将供给侧确定为未满足避孕需求的关键来源以及改善最有效避孕形式可及性的有前景的政策目标。
