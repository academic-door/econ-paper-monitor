美国海关各区关税传导：来自连续贸易冲击的产品层面证据

Tariff Transmission Across US Customs Districts: Product‐Level Evidence From Successive Trade Shocks

摘要

This paper asks how US tariffs on Chinese imports reshape trade across the nation's customs districts, and whether a district's earlier reliance on China shapes how deeply its imports fall. We build a monthly panel of HS6‐level US imports from eleven source countries arriving through eight customs districts between January 2017 and December 2025, and estimate product‐specific import‐demand elasticities by Poisson Pseudo‐Maximum Likelihood with three‐way high‐dimensional fixed effects. The estimated elasticity, which we interpret as a conditional import‐demand response among observed foreign suppliers rather than a fully structural Armington parameter, is substantially larger than earlier nationally aggregated studies have reported. This gap matters because it suggests that aggregation across entry points and product categories has long hidden how severe the trade destruction really is. Districts heavily dependent on Chinese imports before the tariff wave experience much sharper declines than less‐exposed ports. The pattern is sharpest for differentiated goods in which Chinese suppliers had already dominated before the tariff hit, where no obvious alternative source stands ready to absorb displaced demand. A further decomposition separates the contemporaneous response from anticipatory pre‐positioning and shows that a nontrivial share of what has been called the static elasticity actually reflects firms acting on expectations of future tariffs. Trade diversion flows mainly towards ASEAN economies, and distance plays essentially no role.

中文摘要

本文探讨了美国对华进口关税如何重塑全国各海关地区的贸易格局，以及某一地区早期对中国的依赖程度是否影响其进口下降的深度。我们构建了一个月度面板数据集，涵盖2017年1月至2025年12月期间，来自11个来源国、经由8个海关地区进入美国的HS6级进口数据，并采用带有三维高维固定效应的泊松伪极大似然法估计产品层面的进口需求弹性。我们所估计的弹性，被解释为观测到的外国供应商之间的条件性进口需求反应，而非完全结构化的阿明顿参数，其数值显著大于此前全国性汇总研究的结果。这一差异至关重要，因为它表明，跨入境点和产品类别的汇总长期以来掩盖了贸易破坏的严重程度。在关税浪潮前高度依赖中国进口的地区，其进口下降幅度远大于暴露程度较低的港口。这一模式在差异化产品中最为显著，尤其是那些中国供应商在关税冲击前已占据主导地位的产品，这些产品缺乏明显的替代来源来吸收被转移的需求。进一步的分解将同期反应与预期性提前备货区分开来，并表明所谓的静态弹性中，有相当一部分实际上反映了企业基于对未来关税的预期而采取的行动。贸易转移主要流向东盟经济体，而距离因素几乎不起作用。
