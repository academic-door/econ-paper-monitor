农林业在土地利用与土地覆盖变化中的经济学分析：坦桑尼亚巴加-基西马贡贾生态廊道案例研究

Analyzing the economics of agroforestry within land use and land cover change: The case of the Baga-Kisimagonja ecological corridor of Tanzania

摘要

Deforestation poses significant ecological, economic, and social challenges globally. The magnitude of deforestation is, however, spatially heterogeneous, arguable to be higher in the productive agro-ecological landscapes such as in ecological corridors. Agroforestry systems and specifically Silvo-arable, offer a promising solution by balancing agricultural productivity with conservation goals. Their adoption, however, remains restrained due to limited awareness on economic viability and perceived risks among farmers. This study assessed the land use land cover changes of Baga Kisimagonja ecological corridor (BKG) in Tanzania between 1992 and 2022. The study further employs a probabilistic cost-benefit analysis (PCBA) to assess the economic viability of traditional agroforestry practices in comparison to conventional crop farming. The findings indicate that over the study period, crop land expanded at an annual rate of about three to six percent while agroforestry grew at a rate of less than three percent. Forest land decreased by an average of two percent per annum. Such land use dynamics is associated to a net carbon loss of 2.3–3.5 kt C year−1, valued at approximately 18771–32681 USD year−1. Additionally, our analysis showed that agroforestry systems are economically viable compared to conventional crop farming and more so when carbon benefits are integrated. We conclude that agroforestry has the potential to mitigate deforestation and enhance long-term land-use resilience if implemented effectively. The findings of this study are important to guide policymakers, farmers, and conservation organizations in making informed investments in agroforestry technologies and practices to restore the degraded ecosystems.

中文摘要

森林砍伐在全球范围内带来了严峻的生态、经济和社会挑战。然而，森林砍伐的程度在空间上存在异质性，在生产力较高的农业生态景观中，如生态廊道，其程度可能更为严重。农林复合系统，特别是林农复合经营模式，通过平衡农业生产与保护目标，提供了一种有前景的解决方案。然而，由于农民对经济可行性的认识有限及感知风险的存在，其采用仍受到制约。本研究评估了1992年至2022年间坦桑尼亚巴加基斯马贡贾生态廊道（BKG）的土地利用/土地覆盖变化。研究进一步采用概率成本效益分析（PCBA），评估传统农林复合实践相较于传统作物种植的经济可行性。研究结果表明，在研究期间，耕地面积以每年约3%至6%的速度扩张，而农林复合面积增长率低于3%。森林面积年均减少约2%。这种土地利用动态与每年2.3至3.5千吨碳的净碳损失相关，其价值约为每年18771至32681美元。此外，我们的分析显示，农林复合系统在经济上比传统作物种植更具可行性，且在纳入碳效益后尤为显著。我们得出结论，若有效实施，农林复合具有减缓森林砍伐和增强长期土地利用韧性的潜力。本研究的发现对于指导政策制定者、农民和保护组织在农林复合技术和实践方面做出明智投资，以恢复退化生态系统具有重要意义。
