机构差异对儿童虐待举报的影响

Institutional Variation in Child Maltreatment Reporting

摘要

Involvement with the child welfare system varies dramatically across places. While some of this variation may reflect differences in underlying maltreatment risk, it may also result from differences in institutional responses to similar circumstances. To disentangle these explanations, we study Michigan public school data linked to Child Protective Services reports from 2017–2024. School-level reporting rates range from virtually none to more than half of students reported between kindergarten and fifth grade. We estimate school-specific reporting tendencies using value-added methods and document substantial variation in reporting behavior after accounting for differences in student risk. Using student movers, we show that these estimates are forecast-unbiased. Our estimates imply that school-specific reporting practices account for roughly 40 percent of the variation in reporting rates across schools. We then estimate a structural model that decomposes reporting practices into diagnostic skill and preferences. Differences in skill explain more than twice as much variation as differences in preferences, and higher-reporting schools tend to be more skilled at identifying high-risk cases. Counterfactual simulations imply that improving detection skill would yield significant welfare gains, while recent policy proposals aimed at reducing or equalizing reporting rates generally lower welfare by increasing the number of high-risk cases that go unreported.

中文摘要

儿童福利系统介入率在不同地区间存在显著差异。部分差异可能反映了潜在虐待风险的差异，但也可能源于机构对相似情境应对方式的不同。为厘清这些解释，我们研究了2017至2024年间密歇根州公立学校数据，并将其与儿童保护服务报告相关联。学校层面的报告率从几乎为零到幼儿园至五年级期间超过半数学生被报告不等。我们运用增值法估计学校特定的报告倾向，并在控制学生风险差异后，记录了报告行为的显著变异。利用学生流动样本，我们证明这些估计具有预测无偏性。我们的估计表明，学校特定报告实践约解释了学校间报告率变异的40%。随后，我们估计了一个结构模型，将报告实践分解为诊断技能与偏好。技能差异所解释的变异是偏好差异的两倍以上，且报告率较高的学校往往更擅长识别高风险案例。反事实模拟显示，提升检测技能将带来显著的福利改善，而近期旨在降低或均等化报告率的政策提案，则通常因增加高风险案例未被报告的数量而降低福利。
