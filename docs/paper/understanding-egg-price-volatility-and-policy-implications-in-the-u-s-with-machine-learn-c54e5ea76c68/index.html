美国鸡蛋价格波动及其政策含义的机器学习分析

Understanding Egg Price Volatility and Policy Implications in the U.S. With Machine Learning

Xuemei Zhao, Simon Meister, Lucie Maruejols, Xiaohua Yu

摘要

Eggs are an inexpensive and sustainable source of proteins, but volatility in the U.S. egg prices has intensified in recent years, raising concerns over food affordability and market stability. This study examines the drivers of U.S. egg price dynamics over 2004–2025 using a two‐stage framework that combines LASSO‐based variable selection with an ARIMAX forecasting model. The results reveal strong price persistence, together with significant impacts of energy, feed costs and seasonal demand. The influence of avian influenza is strongly time‐dependent, with limited effects in full‐sample averages but significant impacts during high‐intensity outbreak periods, particularly in the mid‐2010s and after 2022. Out‐of‐sample forecasts indicate that the LASSO‐ARIMAX model reduces forecast errors by more than 60% relative to a univariate ARIMA benchmark. These findings suggest that stabilizing key input costs, strengthening timely epidemiological surveillance, and proactively managing predictable seasonal demand pressures are more effective than relying on any single explanatory factor.

中文摘要

鸡蛋是一种廉价且可持续的蛋白质来源，但近年来美国鸡蛋价格波动加剧，引发了对食品可负担性和市场稳定性的担忧。本研究采用两阶段框架，结合基于LASSO的变量选择与ARIMAX预测模型，考察了2004-2025年间美国鸡蛋价格动态的驱动因素。结果显示，价格具有强持续性，同时能源、饲料成本和季节性需求的影响显著。禽流感的影响具有强烈的时间依赖性：在全样本平均值中影响有限，但在高强度疫情暴发期（尤其是2010年代中期及2022年后）影响显著。样本外预测表明，相较于单变量ARIMA基准模型，LASSO-ARIMAX模型将预测误差降低了60%以上。这些发现表明，稳定关键投入成本、加强及时流行病学监测以及主动管理可预测的季节性需求压力，比依赖任何单一解释因素更为有效。
