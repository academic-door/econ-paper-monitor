劳动力市场结果的遗传率与社会互动：来自美国双胞胎人口的证据

Heritability and Social Interaction in Labor Market Outcomes: Evidence from the Population of Twins in the United States

David Card, Jesse Rothstein, Moises Yi

金融劳动教育企业与产业

摘要

We use data on 44,000 twin pairs observed as adolescents in the 2000 Census, linked to their earnings 20 years later, to study the heritability of labor market outcomes. We extend the Classical Twins Design (CTD), which identifies heritability from contrasts between monozygotic and dizygotic twins, to settings where an analyst does not observe zygosity but can measure outcomes for same-sex and opposite-sex twins. We also allow for the presence of a family- and sex-specific component affecting same-sex siblings but only partly shared by opposite-sex siblings. Our extended model identifies heritability from the difference-in-differences of same-sex vs. opposite-sex twins vs. siblings. The estimates indicate strong heritability (h² approximately 0.36) of log earnings. Using an AKM (Abowd et al., 1999) decomposition that separates the person-specific component of earnings from employer-specific pay premiums, we find that both components are heritable. The heritability of employer pay premiums, however, is mainly driven by same-sex twins who work at the same firm, suggesting that social interactions may lead to an over-estimate of the genetic component in earnings. Excluding siblings who work together leads to estimates of the heritability of log earnings that are 15% lower. Similar biases may be present in CTD-based estimates of heritability for other social outcomes like education.

中文摘要

我们利用2000年人口普查中观察到的44,000对青少年双胞胎数据，并将其与20年后的收入相关联，以研究劳动力市场结果的遗传力。我们扩展了经典双胞胎设计（CTD），该设计通过同卵双胞胎与异卵双胞胎之间的对比来识别遗传力，并将其推广到分析者无法观察到卵性、但能够测量同性双胞胎与异性双胞胎结果的情形。我们还允许存在一个家庭与性别特异的成分，该成分影响同性兄弟姐妹，但仅被异性兄弟姐妹部分共享。我们的扩展模型通过同性双胞胎与异性双胞胎相对于兄弟姐妹的差中之差来识别遗传力。估计结果表明，对数收入具有较强遗传力（h²约为0.36）。使用AKM（Abowd et al., 1999）分解，将收入中个人特异的成分与雇主特异的工资溢价分离后，我们发现这两个成分都具有遗传性。然而，雇主工资溢价的遗传力主要由在同一家公司工作的同性双胞胎驱动，这表明社会互动可能导致对收入中遗传成分的高估。排除在一起工作的兄弟姐妹后，对数收入遗传力的估计值降低了15%。在基于CTD的其他社会结果（如教育）遗传力估计中，也可能存在类似偏误。
