地方尺度可持续发展目标协同与权衡分析：基于中国2063个县的案例研究

Analysis of synergies and trade-offs in sustainable development goals at the local scale: A case study of 2,063 counties in China

Mingming Li, Ming Tian

打开原文页面DOI：10.1016/j.landusepol.2026.108310
来源
Land Use Policy · 期刊论文
首次监测
2026-08-28 21:41

Crossref 元数据日期 2026-08-28 · 来源：Crossref
主题
与中国相关农业与食品发展经济学宏观与货币

摘要

Understanding the potential synergies or trade-offs among the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) is crucial for achieving the 2030 Agenda. This study systematically investigates SDG synergies and trade-offs at the local scale by quantitatively describing the monotonicity and linear characteristics of SDG interactions in Chinese counties from 2000 to 2020 and compares the findings with those of other multiscale studies. The key findings include the following: (1) China's local-scale sustainable development trends show overall improvement, although the SDG2, SDG3, and SDG17 indices exhibit declining trajectories. (2) The majority of SDGs do not have significant interactions. Among the classified interactions, synergies are clearly more common than trade-offs are, and linear interactions are more common than nonlinear interactions are. (3) Spatial location and land development intensity shape SDG trade-offs. Synergies are largely consistent across locations, while adjacent counties show stronger economic–ecological tensions. Counties with higher shares of construction land exhibit more active SDG interactions and intensified trade-offs between economic development and ecological conservation. (4) SDG1, SDG8, SDG9, and SDG17 serve as core goals within the local-scale SDG network, functioning as leverage goals to maximize synergies and advance sustainable development. Additionally, the network relationships among the SDGs tend towards simplification over a 20-year period. (5) The synergies and trade-offs among the SDGs exhibit significant spatial variation across the different scales. Compared with global-scale and regional-scale SDG interactions, local-scale SDG relationships demonstrate stronger spatial heterogeneity and structural stability. Macroscale studies (national, provincial) often reveal overarching trends dominated by structural constraints, whereas microscale synergies or trade-offs are more influenced by local resource endowments, industrial structures, and social foundations.

中文摘要

理解可持续发展目标（SDGs）之间的潜在协同效应或权衡关系对于实现2030年议程至关重要。本研究通过定量描述2000年至2020年中国县域SDG相互作用的单调性和线性特征，系统探讨了地方尺度上的SDG协同与权衡，并将研究结果与其他多尺度研究进行了比较。主要发现如下：（1）中国地方尺度的可持续发展趋势总体改善，尽管SDG2、SDG3和SDG17指数呈现下降轨迹。（2）大多数SDG之间不存在显著的相互作用。在已分类的相互作用中，协同效应明显多于权衡效应，线性相互作用多于非线性相互作用。（3）空间位置和土地开发强度塑造了SDG权衡关系。协同效应在不同区位间基本一致，而相邻县域表现出更强的经济-生态张力。建设用地占比较高的县域表现出更活跃的SDG相互作用，以及经济发展与生态保护之间加剧的权衡。（4）SDG1、SDG8、SDG9和SDG17是地方尺度SDG网络中的核心目标，作为杠杆目标以最大化协同效应并推进可持续发展。此外，SDG之间的网络关系在20年间趋于简化。（5）SDG之间的协同与权衡在不同尺度上表现出显著的空间差异。与全球尺度和区域尺度的SDG相互作用相比，地方尺度的SDG关系表现出更强的空间异质性和结构稳定性。宏观尺度研究（国家、省级）往往揭示以结构性约束为主导的总体趋势，而微观尺度的协同或权衡则更多受到地方资源禀赋、产业结构和社会基础的影响。
