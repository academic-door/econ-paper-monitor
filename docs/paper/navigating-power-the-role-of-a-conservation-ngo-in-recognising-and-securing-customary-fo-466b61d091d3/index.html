驾驭权力：保护非政府组织在承认和保障婆罗洲最后游牧民族传统森林权利中的作用

Navigating power: The role of a conservation NGO in recognising and securing customary forest rights for Borneo’s last nomads

Siswandi Siswandi, Nurhady Sirimorok, Micah R. Fisher, Grace Yee Wong, Maria Brockhaus 等

摘要

The legal recognition of Customary Forest (Hutan Adat) in Indonesia centres around a state-centric understanding of community land tenure. Often presented as a progressive policy, in its support of sedentary-agrarian communities and underlying plantation logic it is agrocentric in its paradigm. This research employs a critical ethnographic approach to analyse the struggles of a community in obtaining legal recognition, namely the last nomadic group of Kalimantan, the Punan Batu. Using an integrated SPA-Cube Framework, this study examines the ways that agrocentric governmentality works to marginalise the Punan, while providing formal state recognition. Indeed, we chart the ways Punan nomadic ontologies are rendered illegible. The analysis begins by examining the power background, revealing historical and structural inequalities rooted in state policies that systematically problematise nomadic ways of life. It then investigates the power delivery strategies of a non-governmental organization Yayasan Konservasi Alam Nusantara (YKAN) in their aims to leverage scientific research and participatory mapping to support recognition. While these tactics succeeded in securing district-level support, they also acted as government technologies to discipline the Punan Batu’s mobilities by redefining them into state-recognisable subjects, re-territorialising their fluid spatial practices and formalising egalitarian social structures and practices. Finally, the analysis explores processes of power adjustment, revealing that interventions provoke retaliation from timber companies and agrarian settlers, entrenching patron-client networks to generate momentum for internal dissent. The first author’s positionality as a YKAN insider enabled rich, three-year-long participatory observation, providing deep access and unique insights. These insider perspectives were tempered through the collaborative research approach and prioritised an overall critical reflexivity in engagement and analysis. Conceptually, this study contributes to environmental governance by demonstrating how the SPA-Cube Framework illuminates the paradoxes of recognition, where empowerment strategies can inadvertently reinforce the very governmental logics they challenge. Practically, it highlights the limitations of current recognition paradigms and offers critical insights for NGOs and policymakers.

中文摘要

印度尼西亚习惯森林（Hutan Adat）的法律承认，其核心在于以国家为中心理解社区土地权属。尽管该政策常被表述为进步性举措，但其在支持定居农耕社区及隐含的种植园逻辑方面，本质上属于农业中心主义范式。本研究采用批判性民族志方法，分析一个社区在争取法律承认过程中的抗争，该社区即加里曼丹最后的游牧群体——普南巴图（Punan Batu）。通过整合SPA-Cube框架，本研究考察了农业中心主义治理术如何在提供正式国家承认的同时，边缘化普南族，并揭示普南族游牧本体论如何被建构为不可辨识。分析首先审视权力背景，揭示植根于国家政策中的历史与结构性不平等，这些政策系统性地将游牧生活方式问题化。继而考察非政府组织——努桑塔拉自然保护基金会（YKAN）在利用科学研究与参与式制图支持承认过程中的权力传递策略。尽管这些策略成功获得了区级支持，但它们同时作为治理技术，通过将普南巴图人重新定义为国家可辨识的主体、对其流动的空间实践进行再领土化，并将其平等主义社会结构与习俗加以正式化，从而规训其流动性。最后，分析探讨权力调适过程，揭示干预措施引发木材公司与农业定居者的反击，强化庇护-依附网络以催生内部异议的动能。第一作者作为YKAN内部人员的身份，使得长达三年的参与式观察得以实现，提供了深度接触与独特洞见。这些内部视角通过协作研究方法加以平衡，并在参与及分析中优先贯彻整体批判性反思。在概念层面，本研究通过展示SPA-Cube框架如何阐明承认的悖论——即赋权策略可能无意中强化其所挑战的治理逻辑——为环境治理作出贡献。在实践层面，本研究凸显了现行承认范式的局限性，并为非政府组织与政策制定者提供批判性洞见。
