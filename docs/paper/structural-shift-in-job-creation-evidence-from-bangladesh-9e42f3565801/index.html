孟加拉国就业创造的结构性转变：证据分析

Structural Shift in Job Creation: Evidence from Bangladesh

Khan, Nazmus Sadat

摘要

This paper examines the relationship between economic growth and employment in Bangladesh, with a comparative perspective involving six other Asian countries. It first tests the validity of Okun’s law for Bangladesh and the selected countries using three distinct autoregressive distributed lag model specifications: a dynamic difference model, a gap model based on the Hodrick-Prescott filter, and a gap model based on the Baxter-King filter. The analysis then estimates the employment elasticity of economic growth for Bangladesh across three periods—2003–10, 2010–17, and 2017–24—while also exploring sectoral variations in employment elasticity within these subperiods. The results reveal that the evidence supporting Okun’s law in Bangladesh is weaker than in the other countries. Furthermore, the sectoral analysis shows substantial shifts in the employment elasticity of economic growth over time: the agriculture sector experienced an increase in the employment elasticity of economic growth from 0.29 in 2003–10 to 0.98 in 2016–24, while the industry sector’s elasticity declined from 0.84 to -0.04 over the same period. The service sector also saw a reduction. A more granular examination of subcategories within the industry and service sectors highlights that accommodation and food services and transportation and storage have demonstrated the strongest employment elasticity, whereas the employment elasticity for the manufacturing sector turned negative, performing the worst among the sectors analyzed. These findings carry significant policy implications, underscoring the importance of prioritizing employment-enhancing growth strategies, identifying and promoting sectors with high job creation potential, and focusing on improving job quality rather than pursuing growth-driven employment policies.

中文摘要

本文考察了孟加拉国经济增长与就业之间的关系，并从比较视角纳入其他六个亚洲国家。文章首先使用三种不同的自回归分布滞后模型设定检验奥肯定律在孟加拉国及所选国家是否成立：动态差分模型、基于Hodrick-Prescott滤波的缺口模型，以及基于Baxter-King滤波的缺口模型。随后，分析估计了孟加拉国在三个时期——2003—10年、2010—17年和2017—24年——的经济增长就业弹性，同时探讨了这些子时期内就业弹性的部门差异。结果表明，支持奥肯定律在孟加拉国成立的证据弱于其他国家。此外，部门分析显示，经济增长的就业弹性随时间发生了显著变化：农业部门经济增长的就业弹性从2003—10年的0.29上升至2016—24年的0.98，而工业部门的弹性在同一时期从0.84下降至-0.04。服务业部门也出现下降。对工业和服务业部门内部子类别的更细致考察突出表明，住宿和餐饮服务以及运输和仓储表现出最强的就业弹性，而制造业部门的就业弹性转为负值，在所分析的部门中表现最差。这些发现具有重要的政策含义，强调了优先采取促进就业的增长战略、识别并促进具有高就业创造潜力的部门，以及关注改善就业质量而非推行以增长驱动就业的政策的重要性。
