沙特阿拉伯CO2排放与PM2.5中的能源与经济动态

Energy and Economy Dynamics in CO2 Emissions and PM2.5: Evidence From Saudi Arabia

摘要

The policy challenge of balancing economic growth with environmental protection remains a major challenge for oil-dependent economies. Previous studies have extensively examined the nexus between economic growth, energy consumption, and carbon emissions. However, the joint long-run and short-run dynamics of CO 2 emissions and PM 2.5 concentrations in Saudi Arabia have received less attention. This difference is vital because policies intended to reduce greenhouse gases may not achieve comparable improvements in local air quality. This study investigates associations between economic growth, oil-based electricity, non-renewable energy, globalization, and these two environmental indicators in Saudi Arabia using annual data. The ARDL method estimates long-run equilibrium and short-run dynamics, with robustness checked via unit-root, structural-break, bounds, and diagnostic tests. Findings indicate that explanatory variables relate differently to the two environmental indicators. In the long run, emissions are positively associated with globalization, while PM 2.5 concentrations show a positive association with oil-based electricity production. Economic growth is negatively associated with CO 2 emissions but positively associated with PM 2.5 concentrations, suggesting carbon efficiency improvements do not necessarily improve local air quality. The error-correction results confirm stable long-run relationships. These results support integrated environmental policies. Accelerating renewable electricity deployment, improving energy efficiency, promoting cleaner investments, and reducing oil-fired electricity generation can support Saudi Arabia's energy transition while addressing carbon mitigation and air quality.

中文摘要

在依赖石油的经济体中，平衡经济增长与环境保护的政策挑战依然是一项重大难题。既有研究已广泛考察经济增长、能源消费与碳排放之间的关系。然而，沙特阿拉伯CO2排放与PM2.5浓度的长期与短期联合动态受到的关注较少。这一差异至关重要，因为旨在减少温室气体的政策未必能在地方空气质量方面取得同等程度的改善。本研究利用年度数据，考察沙特阿拉伯经济增长、基于石油的电力、不可再生能源、全球化与这两项环境指标之间的关联。ARDL方法估计长期均衡与短期动态，并通过单位根检验、结构断点检验、边界检验和诊断检验进行稳健性检验。研究结果表明，解释变量与这两项环境指标的关联方式不同。长期来看，排放与全球化呈正相关，而PM2.5浓度与基于石油的电力生产呈正相关。经济增长与CO2排放呈负相关，但与PM2.5浓度呈正相关，这表明碳效率改善未必能改善地方空气质量。误差修正结果证实了稳定的长期关系。这些结果支持综合性环境政策。加快可再生能源电力部署、提高能源效率、促进更清洁投资以及减少燃油发电，可在应对碳减排与空气质量问题的同时，支持沙特阿拉伯的能源转型。
