紧急土地征收、路网中心性与灾后土地利用政策：来自意大利拉奎拉震后重建的证据

Emergency land take, road-network centrality and post-disaster land-use policy: Evidence from the post-earthquake reconstruction of L’Aquila, Italy

Federico Mara, Cristina Montaldi, Francesco Zullo, Valerio Cutini

摘要

Land-use policy increasingly operates under conditions of shock and uncertainty, yet the mechanisms through which emergency measures produce persistent land take remain underexplored. In this paper, land take refers to land development involving the conversion of natural, semi-natural or agricultural land to artificial uses, often associated with impervious soil cover. The paper examines how post-earthquake road-access restrictions, safety cordons, and infrastructure-and-amenity siting choices interacted with road-network centrality and land-use change in L’Aquila, Italy. Using a configurational approach, namely Space Syntax, on reconstructed pre-earthquake and post-earthquake road networks, we compute segment-based Integration (NAIN) and Choice (NACH) at multiple radii and relate centrality deciles to a 2014 post-event census of commercial and public amenities through nearest-segment association and buffer-based sensitivity tests. Results show a strong spatial alignment between road closures in and around the historic centre, the emergence of high-centrality road corridors, notably Viale della Croce Rossa and the SS17, and the concentration of post-disaster amenities along those corridors and their immediate surroundings. Two peripheral district centres, Torrione and Torretta, also consolidated as locally autonomous service nuclei, reinforcing a more polycentric pattern that only partly aligned with statutory planning objectives. Rather than proposing a formal predictive model, the paper uses single-case evidence to show how road-network diagnostics can help identify corridors where temporary land uses and emergency-related land take are most likely to persist; support decisions on the reopening of strategic road links; and inform time-bound rules for de-commissioning, reuse, regularisation, and development caps on floor area and intensity. The workflow is low-cost, open-data based and transferable to other hazards and city sizes, providing actionable evidence for planners and governments seeking to align post-disaster responses with land-take control objectives.

中文摘要

土地政策日益在冲击与不确定性条件下运作，然而紧急措施如何导致持续性土地占用（land take）的机制仍未得到充分探讨。本文中，土地占用指涉及将自然、半自然或农业用地转化为人工用途的土地开发，通常与不透水地表覆盖相关。本文考察了意大利拉奎拉地震后道路通行限制、安全警戒线以及基础设施与便利设施选址如何与道路网络中心性和土地利用变化相互作用。采用构形方法，即空间句法（Space Syntax），对重建的地震前与地震后道路网络进行分析，我们计算了多半径下的分段整合度（NAIN）和选择度（NACH），并通过最近路段关联和缓冲区敏感性检验，将中心性十分位数与2014年震后商业及公共便利设施普查数据进行关联。结果显示，历史中心及其周边的道路封闭、高中心性道路走廊（尤其是红十字大道和SS17公路）的出现，以及灾后便利设施沿这些走廊及其邻近区域的集中，之间存在显著的空间对应关系。两个外围区域中心——托里奥内和托雷塔——也巩固为地方自治的服务节点，强化了一种仅与法定规划目标部分吻合的多中心格局。本文并未提出正式预测模型，而是利用单案例证据展示道路网络诊断如何有助于识别临时性土地利用和紧急相关土地占用最可能持续存在的走廊；支持战略道路连接重新开放的决策；并为退役、再利用、规范化以及建筑面积和强度开发上限提供有时限的规则依据。该工作流程成本低廉、基于开放数据，并可推广至其他灾害类型和城市规模，为规划者和政府提供可操作证据，以将灾后响应与土地占用控制目标相协调。
