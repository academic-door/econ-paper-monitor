重访21世纪粮食系统中的农业政治经济学

Revisiting the Political Economy of Agriculture in 21st Century Food Systems

Danielle Resnick, Johan Swinnen

农业与食品环境与气候发展经济学宏观与货币

摘要

In 1932, Joseph Duncan argued in the Journal of Agricultural Economics that agricultural economists should engage more explicitly with politics. Nearly a century later, the political economy of food and agriculture has expanded in both scope and complexity. This paper revisits the evolution of political economy perspectives on agricultural policy, tracing the shift from early interest‐based explanations centered on farmers and consumers to broader frameworks that incorporate institutions, ideas and the interactions among diverse policy actors. We argue that contemporary transitions from an agricultural policy paradigm to a food systems one require a further rethinking of political economy analysis. Specifically, contemporary food systems pursue multiple and often competing objectives, including productivity, nutrition, environmental sustainability, resilience and equity, while involving a far wider array of stakeholders, from multinational corporations and transnational advocacy networks to technology firms and global governance institutions. At the same time, rising political polarization, populism, digital misinformation and the fragmentation of multilateral governance increasingly shape how policy preferences are formed, coalitions are constructed, and policy compromises are negotiated. These developments complicate traditional assumptions that preferences are driven primarily by material interests and that institutions can reliably facilitate collective action. We illustrate these changing dynamics through brief case studies of the 2024 European farmer protests, Brazil's Amazon Soy Moratorium and Mexico's genetically modified corn dispute. Each case highlights tensions between national sovereignty, global integration, environmental sustainability, and agricultural livelihoods. We conclude that understanding twenty‐first century food policy requires political economy frameworks that integrate interests, institutions, ideas and the changing geopolitical and informational context in which food system decisions are made.

中文摘要

1932年，约瑟夫·邓肯在《农业经济学杂志》上提出，农业经济学家应更明确地参与政治。近一个世纪后，食品与农业的政治经济学在范围和复杂性上均有所扩展。本文回顾了农业政策政治经济学视角的演变，追溯了从早期以农民和消费者为中心的利益解释，到纳入制度、思想及多元政策行动者互动的更广泛框架的转变。我们认为，当代从农业政策范式向食品系统范式的转型，要求对政治经济学分析进行进一步反思。具体而言，当代食品系统追求多重且往往相互竞争的目标，包括生产力、营养、环境可持续性、韧性和公平，同时涉及从跨国公司、跨国倡导网络到科技企业和全球治理机构等更为广泛的利益相关者。与此同时，日益加剧的政治极化、民粹主义、数字虚假信息以及多边治理的碎片化，正日益影响政策偏好的形成、联盟的构建以及政策妥协的谈判方式。这些发展使传统假设——即偏好主要由物质利益驱动，且制度能够可靠地促进集体行动——变得复杂化。我们通过2024年欧洲农民抗议、巴西亚马逊大豆暂停令以及墨西哥转基因玉米争端的简要案例研究，阐释了这些变化中的动态。每个案例都凸显了国家主权、全球一体化、环境可持续性与农业生计之间的张力。我们得出结论，理解21世纪的食品政策需要整合利益、制度、思想以及食品系统决策所处的不断变化的地缘政治和信息背景的政治经济学框架。
