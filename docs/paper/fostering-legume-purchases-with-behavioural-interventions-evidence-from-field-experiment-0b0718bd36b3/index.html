利用行为干预促进豆类购买：来自线上线下超市实地实验的证据

Fostering legume purchases with behavioural interventions: evidence from field experiments in online and offline supermarkets

Maureen Schulze, Meike Janssen

主题

摘要

• Food policies promote plant-based protein; intake remains far below recommendations. • Supermarkets offer a promising setting to increase plant-based protein consumption. • Studies tested interventions in online and physical retail environments. • Legume sales increased with placement and recipe-based in-store interventions. The protein transition is a crucial element of the transition to sustainable food systems, and both EU and national policies aim to foster the consumption of plant-based proteins. Supermarkets as the primary source of food products in many countries represent a promising environment for translating these policy objectives into practice. With three studies from Denmark, including a representative online survey (n = 838 participants) followed by randomised controlled trials in a real online supermarket in two rounds over 6 weeks (n = 9,093 transactions Round 1; n = 8,706 transactions Round 2) and field experiments in physical brick-and-mortar stores (9 test stores, 13 control stores, 21 days of observation), this research investigates the potential of leveraging behavioural interventions to foster legume purchases in online and offline retail settings. The randomised controlled trials in the online supermarket showed that placement interventions in the vegetable section were effective in increasing sales of legumes by 57%, while placement interventions in the meat section were not effective, suggesting that the strategies known to work for highly processed plant-based meat substitutes cannot be translated to legumes. The field experiments in brick-and-mortar supermarkets confirmed that the vegetable section is a promising intervention point. The multi-layered intervention tested (bundling placement combined with a recipe inspiration poster) significantly increased legume purchases to a similar degree as in the other study. This paper concludes that retailers play a pivotal role in the protein transition; however, their commercial interests may impede the implementation of ambitious in-store interventions promoting legumes, highlighting the need for innovative policy approaches.

中文摘要

• 食品政策倡导植物蛋白，但摄入量仍远低于建议水平。• 超市为增加植物蛋白消费提供了有前景的环境。• 研究测试了在线及实体零售环境中的干预措施。• 通过摆放位置和食谱式店内干预，豆类销售额有所增加。蛋白质转型是向可持续食品体系过渡的关键要素，欧盟及各国政策均旨在促进植物蛋白的消费。作为许多国家食品的主要来源，超市是将这些政策目标付诸实践的有前景环境。本研究基于丹麦的三项研究，包括一项代表性在线调查（n=838名参与者），随后在真实在线超市进行了两轮为期6周的随机对照试验（第一轮n=9,093笔交易；第二轮n=8,706笔交易），并在实体实体店进行了现场实验（9家测试门店，13家对照门店，观察21天），探讨了利用行为干预在线上和线下零售环境中促进豆类购买的潜力。在线超市的随机对照试验显示，蔬菜区的摆放干预有效使豆类销售额增加了57%，而肉类区的摆放干预则无效，这表明适用于高度加工的植物基肉类替代品的策略不能直接应用于豆类。实体超市的现场实验证实，蔬菜区是一个有前景的干预点。测试的多层次干预（捆绑摆放结合食谱灵感海报）显著增加了豆类购买，其效果与其他研究相似。本文得出结论，零售商在蛋白质转型中扮演关键角色；然而，其商业利益可能阻碍实施促进豆类的雄心勃勃的店内干预措施，这凸显了创新政策方法的必要性。
