是什么驱动了中欧和东南欧的价格分化？来自基于流市场耦合的证据

What drives price divergence in Central and South-Eastern Europe? Evidence from flow-based market coupling

Adrienn Selei, Gábor Horváth, András Mezősi, Mária Bartek-Lesi, László Szabó

摘要

Electricity prices in Central and South-Eastern Europe exhibited extreme divergence during the summer of 2024, followed by a period in which price differentials remained persistently higher than in Western Europe. This paper investigates the extent to which flow-based market coupling (FBMC) contributed to these outcomes by constraining cross-zonal trade. Using publicly available data, we conduct a critical network element-level empirical analysis that decomposes hourly price differences into the contributions of individual network constraints. The results show that a small number of network elements account for the majority of observed price spreads between Central and South-Eastern Europe, even after controlling for demand conditions and generation fundamentals. Regression results confirm a strong and statistically significant relationship between the available transfer capacity for commercial flow on key critical network elements (RAM scarcity) and regional price divergence. The findings point to important scope for improving FBMC implementation and underline the importance of stricter enforcement of minimum cross-zonal capacity requirements, improved transparency of capacity calculation, and stronger investment incentives for internal grid reinforcements with cross-border welfare benefits.

中文摘要

2024年夏季，中欧和东南欧的电价出现了极端分化，随后一段时期内，价差持续高于西欧。本文研究了基于潮流的市场耦合（FBMC）通过限制跨分区交易在多大程度上促成了这些结果。利用公开数据，我们开展了一项关键网络元件层面的实证分析，将逐小时价格差异分解为各个网络约束的贡献。结果表明，即使在控制了需求状况和发电基本面之后，少数网络元件仍解释了中欧与东南欧之间观察到的大部分价差。回归结果证实，关键网络元件上商业潮流的可用输电容量（RAM稀缺性）与区域价格分化之间存在强烈且统计显著的关系。研究结果指出了改进FBMC实施的重要空间，并强调了更严格执行最低跨分区容量要求、提高容量计算透明度，以及为具有跨境福利效益的内部电网强化提供更强投资激励的重要性。
