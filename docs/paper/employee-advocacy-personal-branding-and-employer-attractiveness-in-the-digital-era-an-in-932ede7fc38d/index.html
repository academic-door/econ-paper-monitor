数字时代的员工倡导、个人品牌与雇主吸引力：一个整合性概念框架

Employee Advocacy, Personal Branding, and Employer Attractiveness in the Digital Era: An integrative conceptual framework

Fatima Ezzahra Khouiammi (UCA - Université Cadi Ayyad = Cadi Ayyad University [Marrakech]), Zayer Chaimaa (UIC - Université Internationale de Casablanca)

摘要

In the digital era, organizations are increasingly relying on employee-generated communication to shape their employer brand image. This study proposes an integrative conceptual framework that examines the relationships between personal branding, employee advocacy, and employer brand attractiveness, while accounting for the moderating role of social media use. Drawing on Social Identity Theory, identity expressiveness theory and Social Exchange Theory, it argues that employees who actively develop their personal brand enhance their visibility, credibility, and professional image, which motivates them to engage in promotional behaviors that benefit their organization. These promotional behaviors, in turn, influence the organization's external image and strengthen the appeal of the employer brand, defined as the perceived benefits of working for a given organization. Furthermore, this theoretical framework suggests that the use of social media strengthens these ties by providing a platform through which employees can express both their personal identity and organizational identity, and reach a wider audience. Methodologically, this study adopts a theory-based integrative literature review relying on a structured analysis of prior research in the fields of organizational behavior, communication, and employer branding. By integrating previously fragmented lines of research, this study contributes to the literature by highlighting the role of employee advocacy as a key mediating mechanism and the use of social media as a contextual moderator in the relationship between personal branding and employer brand attractiveness. The study concludes that personal branding positively influences employee advocacy, which in turn strengthens employer brand attractiveness, while social media use amplifies these relationships in digital environments.From a managerial perspective, it emphasizes the importance of empowering employees to act as agents of strategic communication in order to enhance the organization's appeal in competitive labor markets. Keywords : Personal branding, Employee advocacy, Employer brand attractiveness, Social media use.

中文摘要

在数字化时代，组织日益依赖员工生成的沟通来塑造其雇主品牌形象。本研究提出了一个综合性的概念框架，探讨个人品牌、员工倡导与雇主品牌吸引力之间的关系，并考虑社交媒体使用的调节作用。基于社会认同理论、身份表达理论和社会交换理论，本研究认为，积极发展个人品牌的员工能够提升其可见度、可信度和专业形象，从而激励他们参与有利于组织的推广行为。这些推广行为反过来影响组织的外部形象，并增强雇主品牌的吸引力，即感知到的在特定组织工作的益处。此外，该理论框架表明，社交媒体的使用通过提供一个平台，使员工能够同时表达个人身份和组织身份，并触达更广泛的受众，从而强化这些联系。在方法论上，本研究采用基于理论的结构化综合文献综述方法，系统分析组织行为学、传播学和雇主品牌领域的先前研究。通过整合以往分散的研究线索，本研究为文献贡献在于强调员工倡导作为关键中介机制，以及社交媒体使用作为个人品牌与雇主品牌吸引力之间关系的情境调节因素。研究得出结论：个人品牌正向影响员工倡导，进而增强雇主品牌吸引力，而社交媒体使用在数字环境中放大了这些关系。从管理视角来看，本研究强调赋权员工作为战略沟通代理人的重要性，以增强组织在竞争激烈的劳动力市场中的吸引力。关键词：个人品牌、员工倡导、雇主品牌吸引力、社交媒体使用。
